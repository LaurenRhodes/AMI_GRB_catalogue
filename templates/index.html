The second Arcminute Microkelvin Imager -- Large Array Gamma-ray burst radio afterglow catalogue

Abstract
We present the second iteration of the Arcminute Microkelvin Imager -- Large Array (AMI--LA) Gamma-ray burst (GRB) radio afterglow catalog.
